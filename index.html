Revision techniques

Read These Before Every Comprehension

These are the key techniques Shauri should apply in the next tests. Read this page before starting practice.

1. Summary First Say: “This passage is about...” before answering questions.
2. Narrator Check Inside story: I/we/my. Outside story: he/she/they/names.
3. Flow of Events Know the start, then what changes, and how it ends.
4. Proof Hunt Point to the exact words before writing your answer.
📝

Short Understanding

  • Who or what is the passage mainly about?
  • What is the main event, problem or idea?
  • Can I explain it in one short sentence?
🎬

Simple Flow

  • At the start...
  • Then...
  • At the end...
  • This helps with sequence and change questions.
🎭

Mood and Feeling

  • Is it funny, worried, excited, proud or chaotic?
  • Find feeling words as proof.
  • Do not guess the mood.
💡

Message or Moral

  • What does the story show or teach?
  • Keep it simple and based on the passage.
  • Do not invent a random lesson.
🔎

Highlighting

  • Highlight only 5–8 useful proof phrases.
  • Names, times, places, reasons, actions.
  • Do not colour the whole passage.
🎯

Exact Answer

  • If it asks “when”, give a time.
  • If it asks “where”, give a place.
  • If it asks “two things”, give two things.

Before test checklist

Shauri’s 60-Second Start Check

Before answering the questions, Shauri should be able to tick these in his head.

✓ I can say what the passage is about in one sentence.
✓ I know who is telling the story: inside narrator or outside narrator.
✓ I know the start, then, and end of the passage.
✓ I know the mood or feeling of the passage.
✓ I highlighted only useful proof, not everything.
✓ For every answer, I can show the proof words.
English skills dojo

Key Skills for the Next Tests

📚

Vocabulary in Context

  • Read the sentence before and after.
  • Try the answer inside the sentence.
  • Choose the closest meaning, not the biggest word.
🧠

Inference

  • Inference means working it out from clues.
  • Use: clue + proof = answer.
  • Do not invent feelings or personality.
🧭

Sequence

  • Look for first, then, after, finally.
  • Track what happens in order.
  • Do not mix up during and after.
⚡

Contrast

  • Contrast means opposite or sharp change.
  • Examples: noise to silence, light to darkness.
  • Look for but, however, although.
Parent review

How Parent Should Check

After Shauri completes a passage, check both understanding and answers. This makes the practice powerful.

✅

Understanding Score

  • Narrator correct?
  • Main idea correct?
  • Flow of events correct?
  • Mood and moral reasonable?
  • Highlights useful?
🔍

Answer Score

  • Answer correct?
  • Proof found?
  • Copied accurately?
  • Answered exactly what was asked?
  • Did not write too much?
Parent line to use:

“Show me the exact words that prove your answer.”
