Techniques Shauri Must Remember

📝

Summary First

  • Say: “This passage is about...”
  • If you cannot summarise, read again.
  • Summary stops random guessing.
👀

Narrator Check

  • Inside story: I, me, my, we, our.
  • Outside story: he, she, they, names.
  • This tells you who is speaking.
🎬

Flow of Events

  • At the start...
  • Then...
  • At the end...
  • This helps sequence questions.
🔎

Proof Hunt

  • Point to the exact proof words.
  • Do not choose what only sounds nice.
  • Proof wins. Guessing loses.
🎭

Mood and Feeling

  • Funny, worried, excited, proud, chaotic?
  • Find words that show the feeling.
  • Do not invent the mood.
📚

Vocabulary in Context

  • Read before and after the word.
  • Try each option in the sentence.
  • Choose the closest meaning.
🧠

Inference

  • Inference means working it out.
  • Use clue + proof = answer.
  • Never invent a personality trait.
🎯

Exact Answer

  • When = time.
  • Where = place.
  • Two things = two things.
  • Answer exactly what is asked.
