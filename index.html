Power skills

Shauri’s English Power-Ups

Read these before practice. These are the skills that unlock higher comprehension marks.

📝

Summary First

  • Say: “This passage is about...”
  • Find the main event, problem or idea.
  • If you cannot summarise, read again.
👀

Narrator Check

  • First person: I, me, my, we, our.
  • Third person: he, she, they, names.
  • Ask: who is telling the story?
🎬

Flow of Events

  • At the start...
  • Then...
  • At the end...
  • Great for sequence questions.
🔎

Proof Hunt

  • Point to exact words in the text.
  • Do not choose what only sounds nice.
  • Proof beats guessing every time.
🧠

Inference

  • Inference means working it out from clues.
  • Use: clue + proof = answer.
  • Never invent a feeling or personality.
📚

Vocabulary in Context

  • Read the sentence before and after.
  • Try each option in the sentence.
  • Choose the closest meaning.
🌈

Metaphor

  • One thing is described as another thing.
  • Example: “The classroom was a zoo.”
  • Ask: what picture or feeling does it create?
🧍

Personification

  • A thing is given human actions or feelings.
  • Example: “The wind whispered.”
  • Ask: why did the writer make it seem alive?
✨

Simile

  • Compares using like or as.
  • Example: “as quiet as a mouse.”
  • Ask: what does the comparison show?
🎭

Mood and Tone

  • Funny, tense, excited, sad, calm?
  • Find words that create the feeling.
  • Do not guess. Prove the mood.
⚡

Contrast

  • Contrast means opposite or sharp change.
  • Example: noise to silence.
  • Look for but, however, although.
🎯

Exact Answer

  • When = time.
  • Where = place.
  • Two things = two things.
  • Answer what is asked.

How Shauri Should Use This Before a Test

Goal: remember which skill to use.

Step 2 — Read the Passage

Goal: understand before answering.

Step 3 — Highlight Proof

Highlight only 5–8 useful phrases.

Goal: no over-highlighting.

Step 4 — Answer

Every answer must have proof from the text.

Goal: no random guessing.

Step 5 — Review Mistakes

Ask: what trap caught me?

Goal: mistake becomes XP.

Step 6 — Level Up

Write one rule for next time.

Goal: apply it in the next passage.
